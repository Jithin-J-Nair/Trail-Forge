# User Personas — TrailForge

## Persona 1: Maya — The Independent Inbound Traveler

- **Age / Location:** 29, Berlin; visiting Japan for 10 days
- **Tech profile:** Heavy Google Maps/Instagram/TikTok user; has tried ChatGPT for itineraries but doesn't trust it
- **Goals:** Maximize limited time; discover authentic spots; avoid tourist traps; navigate language barriers
- **Pain points:** AI itineraries include closed or nonexistent places; blogs are outdated; planning takes hours across multiple tabs
- **Jobs to be done:** "Give me a realistic half-day plan I can follow without second-guessing."
- **TrailForge value:** Verified locations, walking-time realism, multilingual descriptions, export to Google Maps
- **Success signal:** Generates 3+ itineraries before trip; follows route in-field; shares exported itinerary
- **Willingness to pay:** $12/mo around travel periods

## Persona 2: Kenji — The Local Experience Curator / Creator

- **Age / Location:** 35, Tokyo; travel content creator and weekend guide
- **Tech profile:** Uses Notion, Google My Maps, CapCut, ChatGPT; publishes routes on social/blog
- **Goals:** Produce high-quality themed routes quickly; maintain credibility with audience; monetize content
- **Pain points:** Manual fact-checking is slow; embarrassment from recommending wrong/closed places; hard to scale content production
- **Jobs to be done:** "Help me draft a verified themed route I can publish with confidence."
- **TrailForge value:** Source-grounded claims, audit trail, multilingual output, export formats, version history
- **Success signal:** Publishes TrailForge-assisted routes weekly; low correction rate; subscriber growth
- **Willingness to pay:** $29/mo Voyager

## Persona 3: Aiko — Boutique Hotel Concierge / Operations Lead

- **Age / Location:** 42, Kyoto; manages guest experience at a 30-room boutique hotel
- **Tech profile:** Uses PMS, LINE, Google Workspace; limited technical staff
- **Goals:** Provide personalized guest itineraries; reduce concierge prep time; improve guest satisfaction scores
- **Pain points:** Staff spend 30-60 min per custom itinerary; inconsistent quality across staff; hard to keep local info current
- **Jobs to be done:** "Generate a personalized, trustworthy walking plan for a guest in 2 minutes."
- **TrailForge value:** White-label/Team workspace, verified local POIs, multilingual guest exports, brand templates
- **Success signal:** Itinerary prep time <5 min; guest NPS lift; staff adoption across shifts
- **Willingness to pay:** $79/seat or $2k/mo white-label

## Persona 4: Daniel — Travel Platform Product Manager (B2B API Buyer)

- **Age / Location:** 38, Singapore; PM at a regional OTA/travel app
- **Tech profile:** Technical PM; evaluates APIs; cares about SLA, compliance, integration effort
- **Goals:** Add AI itinerary feature without hallucination liability; ship fast; maintain brand trust
- **Pain points:** LLM features generate plausible but wrong POIs; legal/compliance wary of user harm; building geo-verification in-house is expensive
- **Jobs to be done:** "Integrate a verified itinerary API with auditable outputs and clear confidence signals."
- **TrailForge value:** Verification API, confidence scores, audit trail, SLA, enterprise security posture
- **Success signal:** Successful pilot in 4 weeks; <1% location error rate in production; conversion lift on itinerary feature
- **Willingness to pay:** $12k–$100k ACV

## Persona 5: Lina — Destination Marketing Organization Analyst

- **Age / Location:** 45, Osaka; DMO digital strategy
- **Tech profile:** Uses GIS tools occasionally, dashboards, spreadsheets; procurement-driven buyer
- **Goals:** Promote lesser-known verified attractions; measure visitor dispersion; ensure information accuracy
- **Pain points:** Outdated destination content across channels; over-tourism concentration; no auditability for published recommendations
- **Jobs to be done:** "Publish accurate, verifiable routes that disperse visitors beyond hotspots."
- **TrailForge value:** Verified content, analytics, area-based route generation, provenance/audit exports
- **Success signal:** Published routes with verified provenance; dispersion metrics improved; reduced content correction cycles
- **Willingness to pay:** $15k–$100k/yr license
